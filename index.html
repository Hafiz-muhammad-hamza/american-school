ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

WHY ALIS?

Since its inception in 1984, American Lyceum International School (ALIS) has been a beacon of quality education. As an international brand, we have consistently delivered top notch educational services, earning the trust and respect of parents and students alike.

At ALIS, we believe in nurturing the whole child. Our educational philosophy is built around the concept ofWhole Brain Teaching, which ensures that students engage both the left and right hemispheres of their brains. This approach enhances cognitive abilities and fosters creativity and critical thinking.

American Lyceum International School (ALIS) offers specialized programs in Programming and Robotics, equipping students with the skills they need to thrive in the digital age. Our curriculum is designed to make learning fun and interactive, encouraging students to explore and experiment.

We understand that education is not just about academics. Emotional and physical development are equally important. Our programs support students' emotional well-being, helping them build resilience and empathy. Physical education promotes a healthy and active lifestyle.

Our curriculum integrates STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) and STEAM (adding Arts to STEM) principles, providing a balanced and comprehensive educational experience.

Choosing American Lyceum International School (ALIS) means choosing a school that is committed to excellence. Our experienced faculty, state-of-the-art facilities, and innovative programs ensure that your child receives the best possible education.
